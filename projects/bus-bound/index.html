bus bound

Bus Bound is a driving simulation set in Emberville, released on April 30, 2026. Players drive officially licensed buses, unlock routes, and upgrade stops to expand the city's transit network. I worked on game and systems design and also contributed to DLC development.

role & project details

  • Role: Game & System Design
  • Genre: Simulator
  • Team Size: 30+
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 4
  • Duration: 1 year +

the problem

Bus Bound had to make individual bus runs feel like part of improving Emberville's transit network. Each run contributes to longer-term progress, from unlocking new routes to upgrading stops. The systems also needed to support future DLC.

what i built

i worked in both early and late development, oversaw the creation of several integral gameplay mechanics, contributed to an unannounced dlc, and supported the launch work for multiple platforms.
